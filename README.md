## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as a Business Analyst assessment for **Ctruh Technology**. The objective was to design a CEO-level dashboard that provides a unified view of business performance across Sales, Marketing, Product, Customer Success, Finance, HR, and Operations, enabling executives to make data-driven decisions quickly.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

> **"I want one dashboard that tells me if the company is healthy — and what I should act on this week."**

The dashboard was designed to provide executives with key business insights within a five-minute review.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- Sales pipeline increased while Win Rate declined, indicating lower conversion efficiency.
- Marketing generated quality leads, requiring stronger Sales–Marketing alignment.
- Product adoption remained healthy, but critical bugs may impact customer retention.
- Financial performance remained stable with healthy MRR and runway.
- Manual reporting presents an opportunity for operational automation.
